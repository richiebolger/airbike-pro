06 · FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which air bikes work with AirBike Pro?

Rogue Echo Bike, AssaultBike Elite, Schwinn Airdyne Pro, Concept2 BikeErg and Wahoo KICKR Bike — plus any bike that broadcasts Bluetooth FTMS, CSCS or CPS. If your bike has Bluetooth, it almost certainly works.

Does the Rogue Echo bike have Bluetooth?

Yes — the Rogue Echo console broadcasts over Bluetooth FTMS. AirBike Pro pairs with it in seconds and streams live power, cadence and speed straight to your iPhone or iPad.

Do I need a subscription?

No. No subscription, no account, no ads. Buy once, own it forever — we think fitness apps should work like the equipment they connect to.

Can I test FTP and train with power zones on an air bike?

Yes — that's the whole point. AirBike Pro includes 20-minute FTP, Ramp, VO₂max and 6-minute max tests, calculates your 7 power zones automatically, and tracks CTL/ATL/TSB, ACWR and readiness over time.

Where does my training data live?

On your device. No cloud sync, no account, nothing leaves your phone. Privacy first.

Does it track heart rate?

Yes — pair any Bluetooth chest strap or HR monitor for live heart rate, HR zones and VO₂max estimation alongside your power data.
