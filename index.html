Live Power Metrics

Connect via Bluetooth and see watts, cadence, speed, and heart rate in real time with colour-coded power zones.

📊

Training Analytics

TSS, Normalized Power, Intensity Factor, and Chronic Training Load — all calculated on-device, nothing sent anywhere.

🏋️

21 Structured Workouts

Built-in HIIT, Threshold, VO₂ Max, Fight Camp, Norwegian 4×4, and more. Build custom intervals or generate a 4-week training plan.

Training History

Calendar view with TSS-coded days, weekly load charts, personal bests, and power curves. Export any session as CSV.

👥

Team / Gym Mode

Run class-style sessions with multiple bikes on one screen. Live power, calories, and HR for every rider — ideal for coaches.

Private by Design

All data stays on your device. No analytics, no ads, no account required. Optional sync to Strava, intervals.icu, and Google Sheets.
